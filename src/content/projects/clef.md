Clef was a concept for a platform where fans could buy shares in emerging music artists — invest early, benefit if they blow up.

The idea: artists get funding without signing away their rights to a label, and fans get a financial stake in the music they believe in. Think of it like an early-stage investment marketplace, but for artists instead of startups.

This never made it past the concept and landing page stage, but the design work and technical spec were fun to put together. Built the landing page in Webflow with a Flutter prototype for the mobile app.

[Source code on GitHub](https://github.com/dgrah50/clef).
