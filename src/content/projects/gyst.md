A Chrome extension that replaces your new tab page with a productivity dashboard. Notes, todos, journaling, and website blocking — all in one place.

I wanted a single tool that did the basics well instead of switching between five different apps.

### Features

- **Note-taking** — capture and tag notes while browsing, searchable later
- **Website blocking** — custom block lists and schedules to keep distractions away during work hours
- **Todo list** — simple task management, always visible
- **Journal** — daily logs right in the browser
- **Year progress bar** — a visual reminder of how much of the year is gone (surprisingly motivating)
